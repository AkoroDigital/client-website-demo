Services

Every step, from pump to paint.

Four services, one crew, no subcontractors passed between jobs.

01

Water extraction

We start pulling standing water within the hour of arrival, using truck-mounted and portable extractors depending on how much water there is. Carpet, pad, and subfloor get checked for saturation before we move to drying.

From $350
02

Structural drying

Air movers and a dehumidifier go in the same day, positioned and logged so we're not guessing when the space is actually dry. We check moisture readings daily and adjust the setup as walls and floors change.

From $75/day
03

Mold prevention

Anywhere moisture sat for more than 24 to 48 hours gets treated before mold has a chance to start, not after we spot it. Antimicrobial application is included on every drying job, not sold as an upsell.
